Good questions

Know what
you’re getting.

Is this the offline or cloud version?

This website features StorArmor Offline for Windows. Your company records stay on the computer running it. It is not a cloud subscription, shared online database, or browser-based customer portal.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The Windows program includes a free demo with fictional records, separate from real company data. Download the installer in the free demo section above. Demo changes do not transfer into your licensed company.

Where do I buy it?

Buy the Windows edition for $299 USD on StorArmor’s Payhip product page. It includes the installer and user manual.

Are there monthly software fees?

StorArmor Offline costs $299 USD as a one-time purchase. There is no monthly software subscription. One purchased license covers one Windows computer in use at a time.

How do I activate the software?

After trying the demo, choose Buy and activate this computer. If you already purchased, enter your Payhip license key and receipt email under I already purchased. Activation needs internet and is tied to this computer.

Can I manage more than one facility?

Yes. StorArmor supports multiple facility records and facility-specific views. In the Offline edition, those records are managed on the same computer; they are not automatically synchronized between offices.

Does it process cards or control my gate?

No. Offline records cash, check, and money-order payments taken outside the app. Integrated card processing, autopay, gate control, public online rentals, and email or SMS delivery are not included in this edition.

What happens if I replace my computer?

Keep a current encrypted backup and its password. Moving to a replacement computer requires a replacement activation and restoring your backup. One purchased license covers one computer in use at a time. Automatic replacement activation is not currently available.

Can I bring in existing tenant records?

You can review and import tenant data from CSV, TSV, and XLSX files. Review the proposed records before applying an import and keep a backup of your source information.
