Good questions

Know what
you’re getting.

Is this the offline or cloud version?

This website features StorVaultOS Offline for Windows. Your company records stay on the computer running it. It is not a cloud subscription, shared online database, or browser-based customer portal.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The Windows program includes a free demo with fictional records, separate from real company data. The public download will be linked here when it is ready. Demo changes do not transfer into your licensed company.

Where do I buy it?

Purchases will be handled through Payhip. Checkout is being prepared; this page will show the verified product link when sales open.

How do I activate the software?

Each activation is matched to the installation on your computer. The current manual process uses the installation request from the app to issue an activation code, which the app verifies locally. Automatic purchase activation is being prepared and is not yet available.

Can I manage more than one facility?

Yes. StorVaultOS supports multiple facility records and facility-specific views. In the Offline edition, those records are managed on the same computer; they are not automatically synchronized between offices.

Does it process cards or control my gate?

No. Offline records cash, check, and money-order payments taken outside the app. Integrated card processing, autopay, gate control, public online rentals, and email or SMS delivery are not included in this edition.

What happens if I replace my computer?

Keep a current encrypted backup and its password. Moving to a replacement computer requires a replacement activation and restoring your backup. One purchased license covers one computer in use at a time. Automatic replacement activation is not currently available.

Can I bring in existing tenant records?

You can review and import tenant data from CSV, TSV, and XLSX files. Review the proposed records before applying an import and keep a backup of your source information.
